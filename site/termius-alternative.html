CYBERDOG VS TERMIUS

A free, open-source alternative to Termius

Termius is a polished SSH client with apps for every device. If what you need on the desktop is a terminal, a real file explorer and a quick view of how your servers are doing, without a subscription, Cyberdog covers it for free. It is open source (MIT), needs no account, and keeps your passwords and keys on your computer.

macOS, Windows & Linux · Free · MIT

Choose Cyberdog if…

  • You want a free tool: no subscription tier, and team sharing included at no cost.
  • You read, edit and move remote files in a real explorer with split view, search and a permissions editor.
  • You want server health, systemd services, live logs and Docker next to the terminal.
  • You prefer open source you can audit and build yourself.

Stay with Termius if…

  • You also work from a phone or tablet: Termius has iOS and Android apps, Cyberdog is desktop only.
  • You want your hosts and keys synced across all your devices through one account.
  • You need port forwarding, or host chaining across several hops. Cyberdog has no port forwarding and supports a single bastion hop.
  • You want a commercial product with vendor support.

What Cyberdog looks like

Moving from Termius

Termius keeps its hosts in an encrypted database with no export, so Cyberdog cannot import them. It does import Cyberduck, FileZilla, WinSCP, Transmit, WebSSH, MobaXterm, SecureCRT, PuTTY, Tabby, rclone and your SSH config (the one VS Code Remote-SSH uses). Add your servers with a name, host, login and either a password or an SSH key. You can generate keys, install the public key on a server and organize everything in groups.

Questions

Can Cyberdog fully replace Termius?

On the desktop, for SSH, SFTP and server monitoring, usually yes. It does not replace Termius if you need mobile apps, cross-device sync, port forwarding or multi-hop host chaining.

Where are my passwords and keys stored?

Passwords are encrypted with your operating system keychain and private keys stay on your computer. The optional account only stores what you choose to share with a team (name, host, port and username). Syncing SSH credentials for a shared server is opt-in.

Is team sharing really free?

Yes. Teams, roles, invitation links, shared scripts and server history are free. Everything runs on an optional account; Cyberdog works without one.

Is Cyberdog affiliated with Termius?

No. Cyberdog is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Termius.
